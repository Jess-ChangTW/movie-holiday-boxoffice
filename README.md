# Do Holidays Sell Movie Tickets?

**How Halloween and Valentine's Day shape release scheduling and US box office, 2010-2025.**

[Interactive Tableau story](TABLEAU_PUBLIC_LINK) · [Findings log](findings.md) · Python, pandas, statsmodels, Tableau

---

## Key findings

1. **Studios cluster horror before Halloween.** Horror's share of weekly releases jumps from a 20% yearly average to 30-34% in the five weeks before Halloween, then falls to the year's lowest right after. Romance clusters in the week before Valentine's Day (smaller effect, still significant: 17.5% vs 13.3%, p = 0.001).
2. **Matched genres gain a relative advantage.** In the Halloween window, horror's median US gross is $36.7M vs $22.8M at other times, while the median for all other films falls from $24.1M to $11.3M. October and early February are weak weeks for most films; the matched genre bucks the trend.
3. **About half the raw premium is selection, half is the holiday.** Bigger-budget and franchise films are scheduled into holiday windows. After controlling for budget, franchise, MPA rating, IMDb rating, year and month, a premium of about **+50-60%** remains for both horror (p ≈ 0.01) and romance (p ≈ 0.04).
4. **Raw timing patterns can mislead.** Horror released 2-3 weeks before Halloween looks best in raw medians ($53.9M), but the gap disappears after controls: studios simply place their biggest horror films there.

## Data

| Source | Used for | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| [TMDB API](https://www.themoviedb.org/documentation/api) | Film list, US theatrical dates, genres, budget, franchise, rating | 56,393 US theatrical titles pulled, 11,227 after cleaning |
| [OMDb API](https://www.omdbapi.com/) (CC BY-NC 4.0) | US domestic gross, IMDb genre and rating | 4,761 films with US gross |

Box Office Mojo was considered but not used because its robots.txt disallows automated access.

**Main analysis sample: 2,636 films** with US gross of at least $1M, excluding re-releases. Horror and romance use a strict definition (tagged by both TMDB and IMDb).

## Method

- **Scheduling (Layers 1-2):** genre x week-of-year release shares, days-to-holiday distributions, two-proportion z-tests.
- **Box office (Layer 3):** window vs rest-of-year medians with bootstrap 95% CIs; difference-in-differences on log gross (genre premium minus the window effect on other films); robustness checks with alternative windows, date agreement, gross cutoffs and pandemic exclusion.
- **Controls (Layer 4):** OLS on log gross with a genre x window interaction, adding year FE, budget, franchise, MPA rating, IMDb rating and month FE step by step; robust (HC1) standard errors.

### Data quality decisions

- Replaced the TMDB vote-count filter with box office as the definition of a real US theatrical release, which fixed a declining film-count bias in TMDB.
- Flagged and removed re-releases (e.g. Studio Ghibli re-runs) where TMDB and OMDb dates differed by more than a year.
- Used medians and log gross because 8.8% of films earn 62% of total box office.

## Limitations

- Observational data: controls reduce but do not remove the bias from studios choosing release dates.
- Small holiday groups (35-40 matched films per window) give wide intervals.
- IMDb rating is measured after release and may over-control.
- OMDb gross is not inflation-adjusted and has small errors for some titles.

## Repository structure

```
src/            numbered pipeline, run in order
outputs/        charts and auto-generated reports (.md)
tableau/        Tableau workbook (data files are regenerated by step 13)
findings.md     running log of results and decisions
```

| Step | Script | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | `01_fetch_tmdb.py` | Pull US theatrical releases from TMDB |
| 02 | `02_check_data.py` | First data sanity check |
| 03 | `03_fetch_details.py` | Fetch film details (US dates, budget, franchise, IMDb ID) |
| 04 | `04_clean_data.py` | Clean dates, add genre flags and holiday windows |
| 05 | `05_data_overview.py` | Data scope and overview report |
| 06 | `06_release_calendar.py` | Genre x week heatmap, weekly genre share |
| 07 | `07_holiday_timing.py` | Timing, trends, competition, z-tests |
| 08 | `08_fetch_omdb.py` | Fetch US domestic gross from OMDb |
| 09 | `09_merge_boxoffice.py` | Merge box office, flag re-releases, strict genres |
| 10 | `10_quality_check.py` | Date, sample, skew and genre-label checks |
| 11 | `11_boxoffice_comparison.py` | Window comparison, DiD, timing bins, robustness |
| 12 | `12_regression.py` | DiD decomposition and regressions with controls |
| 13 | `13_export_tableau.py` | Export Tableau-ready files |

## Reproduce

```
pip install -r requirements.txt
cp .env.example .env    # add your TMDB and OMDb API keys
python src/01_fetch_tmdb.py    # then run 02 to 13 in order
```

Raw data is not included in this repository; the scripts re-create it from the APIs.
